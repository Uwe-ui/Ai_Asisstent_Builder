## Refactor note: AI selection principle
Choose the best AI **per role**, not one AI for everything.
The best AI is the one that best matches the job:
- strongest reasoning for planning and critical review
- strongest code-specialized and instruction-following model for implementation
- smaller/cheaper structured-output model for classification and routine transforms
- strong reasoning model again for failure analysis and architectural conflicts

If your environment provides exact model names, prefer the highest-quality available model in the relevant category and record the choice in `AI_ROLE_MATRIX.md`.
